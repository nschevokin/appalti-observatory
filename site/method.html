Method and limits

Question

What do Italian public procurement records support saying about how much was awarded, to whom, and by what procedure, and where do they not?

Data

ANAC records in the Open Contracting Data Standard, via the Open Contracting Partnership mirror (CC BY 4.0), pinned by sha256. Only tenders above 40,000 euro are present.

Indicators

  • Awarded value: sum of award amounts in euro. The headline leaves out processes whose awards sum to more than ten times the tender amount (D025); the table shows both.
  • Supplier concentration: share of the headline value held by the five largest suppliers. Awards with several suppliers are split equally (D021). Shown as a range between the identifier join and a merge of ids sharing a legal-form name.
  • Procedure mix: share of value by class of procedure, from the procedure code (D023).
  • Single-bidder share: awards with one tenderer over awards with a published tenderer count. Not computable here: the field is empty.

Two clocks

Every indicator is computed on the award year and on the tender start year (D032). The release year is not used: its counts follow when the publisher loaded records, not when things happened.

Gates

Gate 1 validates every record at ingestion and publishes the rejection counts. Gate 2 judges each pair of consecutive years on populatedness, volume ratio and composition drift against thresholds in gate.toml, fixed before any indicator was computed. A refusal is printed in place of the comparison.

Entities

A supplier is its normalised tax identifier. A hand audit of multi-identifier names measured how often this splits one company; no merge rule met the pre-registered bar, so none is applied.

Limits

  • Awarded, not spent.
  • Above-threshold contracts only.
  • Regions come from postal codes, not from an official field.
  • Tender and award records can be published late or restated; counts for recent years are incomplete.
  • The audit labels were drafted by an AI assistant and reviewed by the author (D036).

Full decisions: decisions log.

What these figures do not mean. Awarded is not spent: the planning, documents and transactions sections of the data are empty, so execution and payment are invisible, and contracts are amended, cancelled and underspent. This is not all public procurement: the threshold is 40,000 euro, so everything below it is absent. A single bidder is not evidence of wrongdoing; it has ordinary explanations such as specialised goods, emergencies, small regional markets and framework call-offs. No page names or ranks a buyer or supplier by any risk indicator.

Gate 1 excluded 5,165 of 562,886 processes (0.92%) before any figure here was computed. The exclusion is concentrated in a small set of buyers and is counted on the data quality page.
