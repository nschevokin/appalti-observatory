What these figures do not mean. Awarded is not spent: the planning, documents and transactions sections of the data are empty, so execution and payment are invisible, and contracts are amended, cancelled and underspent. This is not all public procurement: the threshold is 40,000 euro, so everything below it is absent. A single bidder is not evidence of wrongdoing; it has ordinary explanations such as specialised goods, emergencies, small regional markets and framework call-offs. No page names or ranks a buyer or supplier by any risk indicator.
